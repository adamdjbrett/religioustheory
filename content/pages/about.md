# About Religious Theory

Religious Theory is a review, commentary, and conversations feature of the [Journal for Cultural and Religious Theory](https://jcrt.org).

## Mission

Religious Theory provides critical engagement with contemporary scholarship in religious studies, theology, philosophy, cultural theory, and related fields. We feature:

- Book reviews and essays
- Author reflections and interviews  
- Critical conversations and debates
- Conference announcements and calls for papers

## Topics

Our coverage spans a wide range of subjects including:

- Political theology and theory
- Continental philosophy of religion
- Critical theory and religion
- Cultural studies and religion
- Feminist and gender theory
- Postcolonial and decolonial thought
- Ethics and virtue theory
- Media and aesthetic theory
- And much more
